# Guided Question Templates

Focused, context-aware questions organized by scenario. These are templates, not scripts — adapt to the specific situation.

## Technical/Debugging Scenario

```
Goal:       What are you trying to accomplish?
Context:    What environment/stack are you working in?
Evidence:   What error messages or unexpected behavior do you see?
Attempts:   What have you tried so far, and what were the results?
```

**Lightweight example:**
"Before I dive in — what error message or unexpected behavior are you seeing?"

**Forced Thinking example:**
"I can help debug this, but the fix depends heavily on context. Could you share:
1. What you're trying to accomplish?
2. What error/unexpected behavior you're seeing?
3. What you've already tried?"

## Design/Architecture Scenario

```
Goal:       What problem are you solving? What outcome matters most?
Constraint: What are your non-negotiables (time, cost, scale, team)?
Trade-off:  What have you considered, and what direction are you leaning?
Scope:      What's in scope vs explicitly out of scope?
```

**Lightweight example:**
"To give you the right architecture suggestion — what's your primary constraint: speed of delivery, long-term maintainability, or scale?"

**Forced Thinking example:**
"Architecture recommendations vary widely based on context. To avoid steering you wrong:
1. What problem are you solving, and what outcome matters most?
2. What constraints are non-negotiable (budget, timeline, team size)?
3. Have you evaluated any options already, and what direction are you leaning?"

## Coding Scenario

```
Goal:       What should the code accomplish?
Input/Output: What are the expected inputs and outputs?
Constraint: Performance, security, compatibility requirements?
Context:    Existing codebase patterns, tech stack constraints?
```

**Lightweight example:**
"One quick clarification — what's the expected input/output for this function?"

**Forced Thinking example:**
"Writing this well requires a few details:
1. What exactly should this code accomplish?
2. What are the expected inputs and outputs?
3. Any constraints I should know (performance, security, compatibility)?"

## Learning/Conceptual Scenario

```
Current:    What do you already understand about this topic?
Goal:       What do you want to be able to do with this knowledge?
Angle:      Do you want theory, practical application, or comparison?
Depth:      Overview or deep dive?
```

**Lightweight example:**
"To tailor this explanation — are you looking for practical usage or conceptual understanding?"

**Forced Thinking example:**
"I can explain this at multiple levels. To make it useful:
1. What do you already know about this topic?
2. What do you want to be able to do after learning it?
3. Do you prefer a practical example or theoretical explanation?"
